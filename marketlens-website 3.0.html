Process
How the Analysis Works

Every rating is built from six distinct scoring categories, combined into a weighted formula that ranks every stock or crypto with precision.

01

Six-Category Scoring

Each asset is evaluated across six independent rating dimensions — fundamentals, momentum, technicals, sentiment, risk, and macro context.

02

Weighted Formula

The six scores are fed into a proprietary weighting formula that outputs a single composite score reflecting true market potential.

03

Ranked Lists

Results are published as ranked lists — every stock or crypto ordered by weighted score so you can immediately see what stands out.
